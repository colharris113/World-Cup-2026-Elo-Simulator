🏆 FIFA WORLD CUP 2026 - ELO SIMULATOR 🏆

Elo-based simulation using real FIFA rankings. Home advantage (+175) applied to USA, Mexico, Canada.

🏆 How the World Cup 2026 Simulator Works

📊 Match Engine

Every match uses Elo ratings from real FIFA rankings to determine the probability of each outcome. Goals are generated using a Poisson distribution (like real football analytics), scaled by each team's Elo-based xG.

🏠 Home Advantage

The three host nations (USA, Mexico, Canada) get a +175 Elo boost in the group stage, Round of 32, and Round of 16. From the quarter-finals onward, only the USA keeps the boost (Mexico and Canada don't host those rounds).

⏰ Extra Time & Penalties

Knockout draws go to extra time with a 1.25× Elo difference multiplier ("Fatigue Stretch"). If still level, a penalty shootout gives the first-kicking team a 54% edge. Regulation draws are allowed in the group stage.

⚽ Golden Boot

200 real players have a Goals Per Game (GPG) rating calculated from sportsbook odds. Every time a team scores, a weighted random draw picks the scorer — stars like Mbappé (0.86 GPG) are far more likely to bag than squad players. The top 3 get a podium after the simulation.

🏆 Stats Dashboard

After each simulation you'll see: Cinderella (lowest Elo, deepest run), Fraud Watch (highest Elo, earliest exit), Chaos Magnet (most extra-time matches), Golden Boot / Iron Curtain / Sniper (top goalscorer, best defense, best conversion), plus xG metrics and match superlatives.

🇺🇸 The Donald Trump Annoyance Factor

Tracks how hostile nations (Iran, Haiti, Iraq) and rival nations (Mexico, Canada) perform relative to their Elo expectations — balanced against Team USA's own performance. If Iran wins the whole tournament while the USA crashes out, the Global Spite Rating goes nuclear. Also flags the most notable USA vs. hostile/rival result.

📦 Tin Medal

A dishonorable mention for the highest-GPG golden boot player who scored zero goals across the entire tournament. Someone had to be the bust.
